# Rozpad na pixely / Pixel disintegration 🧩

Fotka sa rozpadne na pixely. Nahraj až 30 fotiek, nastav efekt a ulož video (napr. mesačný recap).
Všetko beží v prehliadači, fotky sa nikam neposielajú.

Turn photos into particles. Upload up to 30 photos, tune the effect and save a video (e.g. a monthly recap).
Everything runs in your browser, photos are never uploaded anywhere.

---

## SK

### Čo to vie
- až 30 fotiek, striedajú sa: jedna sa rozpadne, ďalšia sa poskladá
- formáty: pôvodný, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5, 16:9
- tvary častíc: štvorec, kruh, trojuholník, čiarka, kód
- smer: vietor, od stredu, vír; gravitácia, turbulencia, žiara
- presety: Thanos, Piesok, Iskry, Vír, Matrix
- vlastný text na prvej fotke (fonty, hrúbka, veľkosť, rozostup), ktorý zmizne glitchom
- rozpad prstom: potiahni po fotke a poskladá sa ďalšia v poradí, zvyšok sa opraví glitchom
- uloženie slučky alebo prstového rozpadu ako video (MP4 alebo WebM)
- slovenčina a angličtina (tlačidlo vpravo hore)


## EN

### Features
- up to 30 photos, alternating: one disintegrates, the next assembles
- formats: original, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5, 16:9
- particle shapes: square, circle, triangle, streak, code
- direction: wind, from center, vortex; gravity, turbulence, glow
- presets: Thanos, Sand, Sparks, Vortex, Matrix
- custom text on the first photo (fonts, weight, size, letter spacing) that vanishes with a glitch
- finger mode: drag across the photo and the next one assembles, leftovers are repaired with a glitch
- save the loop or the finger session as video (MP4 or WebM)
- Slovak and English (button in the top ### 


### Notes
- Video recording needs a recent Chrome, Edge or Safari. The browser picks MP4 or WebM.
- Many photos use a lot of memory. The app lowers the resolution for large counts, to at most 1080p per photo.
- Some browsers cannot read HEIC files. Convert them to JPG.
- Fonts load from Google Fonts. Offline, a fallback font is used.
